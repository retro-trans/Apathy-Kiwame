# Credit roll · 0.8.2

The original roll contains seven headings and 37 credited-name rows. All 44
rows are retained in their original order, section and vertical position. The
transparent English SVG uses outlined GenEi Latin lettering at the source's
690 × 4000 dimensions. The native scenario still displays it at 690 × 3500,
animates upward by 3000 pixels over 80000 ms, and returns after its original
60000 ms wait. Backgrounds, filters, music and original PNG remain unchanged.

The original resource attribution placement is preserved, including the
Freepik credit under Sound. No translation-team credit replaces or displaces
an original contributor. Existing Latin identities retain their spelling;
the visual underscores in Chris Zabriskie and Kevin MacLeod become spaces.

The user explicitly approved provisional Latin spellings on 2026-11-14, with
uncertainties recorded here rather than displayed as annotations in the roll.
These six distinct spellings remain unverified:

Verified identities and resource labels use source artwork plus public pages.
The [developer's own blog](https://plaza.rakuten.co.jp/iijimatakiya/diary/202001180000/)
confirms Nozomu Umihara's former Aki Inoue credit; the
[writer's page](https://kakuyomu.jp/works/822139842378830645) supplies the reading.
[Datto Azami's creator profile](https://game-creators.camp/@datto) links the
AzamiDatto portfolio and handle. [Ontama](https://ontama-m.com/) explicitly
states its reading, and [TK's Free Sound FX](https://taira-komori.net/freesounden.html)
provides its own English brand. Other source links and row decisions are in
`work/glossary/credits.json` and `work/translation/en/credits.en.json`.

The enlarged original confirms that the credit previously inventoried as
Onmon is actually Ryumon (竜門). This corrects the transcription, preserving
the credited identity. Kana names such as Kabocha are romanized directly.

Accuracy review compared every row with the original image. Editorial review
kept credited aliases and recognizable resource brands, and proofreading
checked Latin spelling, spacing, duplicate attribution, order and the complete
44-row count. Provisional readings above are the remaining accuracy limits.
No dialogue or scenario text changes, so existing text-flow maps are unchanged.

Native visibility, scroll, title-return and binding evidence is recorded in
`docs/credits-roll-0.8.2-verification.json`. This is a staged local change; the
running main Steam copy and its saves are untouched. No release is published.
